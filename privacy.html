Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-30

The short version

Your routines, step names, schedules, history and proof photo stay on your device and are never sent anywhere. There is no account and no DayPlay server. What leaves the device is a small set of usage counters, crash reports and subscription checks. They are tied to random IDs created for this installation and to a device ID, not to your name or email. You can switch usage counters and crash reports off in Settings.

Who is responsible for your data

Data controller: Halil Berkay Şahin (TheCodevolt), Türkiye. Contact: info@thecodevolt.com. This policy covers the DayPlay iOS app and this website.

What stays on your device only

  • Your routines, steps, planned durations, schedules, alarm settings and reminder preferences.
  • Your run history: which routine ran, when, and how long each step actually took.
  • App preferences: theme, theme pack, language, onboarding state, paused session.
  • Morning proof photos (optional): the reference photo of the spot you chose and its image features. When the alarm rings, the new photo is compared on your phone with Apple's Vision framework. Only its image features may be kept to help future matches; the photo itself is not saved, and nothing is uploaded. The camera is used only for this.

This data lives in the app container. Deleting DayPlay deletes all of it. You can export a copy at any time from Settings, and that export goes wherever you choose to send it. We never receive it.

If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer, that backup includes DayPlay's on-device data, including the proof photo. Apple, not DayPlay, holds those backups.

None of this contains your name, email or an account. It is pseudonymous: the IDs above let a provider tell one installation from another, so privacy law treats it as personal data. We do not use the advertising identifier, we do not track you across other companies' apps, and the App Tracking Transparency prompt is never shown.

Location

DayPlay never asks for your location. Our providers see your IP address when your phone connects to them. From version 1.6.1, Mixpanel is told not to use it for location and DayPlay no longer uses Superwall. Versions up to 1.6 let Mixpanel and Superwall derive an approximate city, region and country from it.

Turning counters off

Settings > Privacy > Usage counters. When it is off, usage counters and crash reports stop. RevenueCat still checks your subscription with Apple, because DayPlay Pro cannot work without it. No other feature changes. The same screen copies the events recorded on your device so you can read them; the providers add device model, iOS version and the IDs above.

How long it is kept

Usage counters are retained for at most 24 months and then deleted. Crash reports are kept by Firebase for 90 days. Subscription records are kept for the life of the subscription plus the period required by tax and consumer protection law. On device data is kept until you delete it or delete the app. Firebase keeps installation IDs until we delete them.

International transfers

Mixpanel processes DayPlay data on servers in the European Union. RevenueCat and Google (Firebase Crashlytics) process data in the United States under Standard Contractual Clauses. Up to version 1.6, Superwall (United States) also received data. Apple processes payments under its own terms.

Your rights

Under the GDPR and, in Türkiye, under KVKK Article 11, you can ask for access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and you can object to processing. Write to info@thecodevolt.com; we answer within 30 days. Because there is no account, the providers keep these records under random IDs that we cannot match to you from an e-mail alone. Write to us anyway: we will delete what we can locate, tell you what we could not, and turning Usage counters off stops new records. You can also complain to your local supervisory authority, or in Türkiye to the KVKK authority (kvkk.gov.tr).

Notifications and alarms

Alarms and reminders are scheduled locally by iOS. There is no push server and no remote notification. If you deny the alarm or notification permission, everything else in the app keeps working.

Purchases

DayPlay Pro is sold through Apple's In App Purchase. Apple processes the payment; we never see your name, card, or Apple ID. RevenueCat receives the purchase receipt and the App Store's app transaction ID from Apple so the app can tell whether Pro is active.

Children

DayPlay is not directed at children under 13 and asks for no personal information from anyone, regardless of age.

Changes

If a future version changes what is collected, this page, the App Store privacy label and the in app privacy text are updated together and before that version ships.
