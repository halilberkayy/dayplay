Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-01

The short version

Your routines, step names, schedules and history stay on your device and are never sent anywhere. There is no account and no DayPlay server. What does leave the device is a small set of anonymous usage counters and subscription events, and you can switch the counters off in Settings.

Who is responsible for your data

Data controller: Halil Berkay Şahin (TheCodevolt), Türkiye. Contact: info@thecodevolt.com. This policy covers the DayPlay iOS app and this website.

What stays on your device only

  • Your routines, steps, planned durations, schedules, alarm settings and reminder preferences.
  • Your run history: which routine ran, when, and how long each step actually took.
  • App preferences: theme, theme pack, language, onboarding state, paused session.

This data lives in the app container. Deleting DayPlay deletes all of it. You can export a copy at any time from Settings, and that export goes wherever you choose to send it. We never receive it.

None of this is linked to a name, an email address, or an account, because DayPlay has no accounts. We do not use the advertising identifier and the App Tracking Transparency prompt is never shown.

Turning counters off

Settings > Privacy > Usage counters. When it is off, nothing is sent, crash reports included. No feature of the app changes. The same screen has a button that copies the exact events recorded on your device so you can read them yourself.

How long it is kept

Usage counters are retained for at most 24 months and then deleted. Crash reports are kept by Firebase for 90 days. Subscription records are kept for the life of the subscription plus the period required by tax and consumer protection law. On device data is kept until you delete it or delete the app.

International transfers

Mixpanel processes DayPlay data on servers in the European Union. RevenueCat, Superwall and Google (Firebase Crashlytics) process data in the United States under Standard Contractual Clauses. Apple processes payments under its own terms.

Your rights

Under the GDPR and, in Türkiye, under KVKK Article 11, you can ask for access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and you can object to processing. Write to info@thecodevolt.com; we answer within 30 days. Because there is no account, please tell us your device model and the approximate first install date so we can locate any records. You can also complain to your local supervisory authority, or in Türkiye to the KVKK authority (kvkk.gov.tr).

Notifications and alarms

Alarms and reminders are scheduled locally by iOS. There is no push server and no remote notification. If you deny the alarm or notification permission, everything else in the app keeps working.

Purchases

DayPlay Pro is sold through Apple's In App Purchase. Apple processes the payment; we never see your name, card, or Apple ID. RevenueCat receives the purchase receipt from Apple so the app can tell whether Pro is active.

Children

DayPlay is not directed at children under 13 and asks for no personal information from anyone, regardless of age.

Changes

If a future version changes what is collected, this page, the App Store privacy label and the in app privacy text are updated together and before that version ships.
