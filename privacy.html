Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-05

The short version

DayPlay collects nothing. There is no account, no server, and no analytics SDK in the app. Everything you create stays in the app's own storage on your device.

Data we collect

None. DayPlay does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. We cannot see your routines, your step names, your timings, or your usage, because none of it is ever sent anywhere.

Data stored on your device

  • Your routines, steps, planned durations, schedules and reminder settings.
  • Your run history: which routine ran, when, and how long each step actually took.
  • App preferences: theme, theme pack, language, onboarding state, paused session.

This data lives in the app container. Deleting DayPlay deletes all of it. You can export a copy at any time from Settings, and that export goes wherever you choose to send it.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally by iOS. There is no push server and no remote notification. If you deny the notification permission, everything else in the app keeps working.

Purchases

DayPlay Pro is sold through Apple's In App Purchase. Apple processes the payment and tells the app whether an entitlement is active. We never see your name, card, or Apple ID. Apple's own privacy policy governs that transaction.

Tracking

DayPlay does not track you across apps or websites, does not use the advertising identifier, and shows no ads. The App Tracking Transparency prompt is never shown because there is nothing to ask for.

Children

DayPlay is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone, regardless of age.

Changes

If a future version ever starts collecting anything, this page, the App Store privacy label, and the in app privacy text will be updated together and before that version ships.
