Common questions

My reminder did not arrive

Check iOS Settings > DayPlay > Notifications. Focus modes, Low Power Mode, and Scheduled Summary can all hold a reminder back. DayPlay schedules reminders locally, so notifications must be allowed for the app itself.

I paid but Pro is not on

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchase. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID used for the purchase. If it still does not appear, email us with the date of purchase.

How do I cancel my subscription?

iOS Settings > your name > Subscriptions > DayPlay, or tap Manage subscription in the app's Settings. Cancelling stops the next renewal.

How do I move my routines to a new phone?

Settings > Export routines (JSON) on the old phone, then Settings > Import on the new one and paste it.

Does DayPlay treat ADHD?

No. It is a focus and routine tool designed to be friendly to ADHD brains. It is not a medical treatment or diagnostic tool.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app. Everything DayPlay stores lives in its own container on your device, so removing the app removes all of it. There is no server copy.
