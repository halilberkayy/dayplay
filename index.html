Play your day

Planning is easy. Starting is hard. DayPlay plays your routines like a music player: one step on screen, a big countdown, one button.

What it does

  • Full screen player. One step at a time, nothing else competing for attention.
  • Honest timing. The summary shows planned versus actual minutes, with no flattery.
  • Pick up where you left off. Leaving mid routine pauses it, it does not erase it.
  • Gentle streaks. No shaming, no guilt language, ever.
  • Lock screen timer. Live Activity and Dynamic Island keep the countdown in view.

Privacy in one line

No account, no server, no analytics. Your routines never leave your phone. Read the full privacy policy.
