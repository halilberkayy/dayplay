The alarm you can't snooze away

Turning the alarm off is easy. Getting up is hard. DayPlay rings a real alarm through Silent mode and Focus, and with mission lock it does not stop until you finish the first step of your routine.

Get DayPlay on the App Store

Not sure yet? Play a three step routine right here, or browse the routine templates. No account, nothing installed.

What makes it different

  • A real alarm, not a notification. Built on Apple's AlarmKit (iOS 26 and later), so it breaks through Silent mode, Focus and Do Not Disturb the same way the Clock app does.
  • Mission lock. The stop button does not send you back to bed, it opens your routine. The alarm keeps ringing until step one is actually done.
  • Counted snoozes. You decide how many snoozes you get. When they run out the snooze button is gone, not greyed out.
  • Escape protection. Force quitting the app while the mission is locked re arms the alarm instead of ending it.
  • Full screen player. After you are up, one step fills the screen with a big countdown and a single button.
  • Honest timing. The summary shows planned versus actual minutes with no flattery.
  • Lock Screen and Dynamic Island. The countdown stays in view, and there is an Apple Watch companion.

Who it is for

People who turn off three alarms in their sleep. People with ADHD, delayed sleep phase, night shifts, or a morning routine that falls apart between the alarm and the first task. DayPlay is not a medical treatment; it is a tool designed around how an unreliable morning actually fails.
